## 内容 1：一件设计，两种展示方式

英文帖文：
One apparel design, two ways to show it.
Export a front + back image for a clear overview, then a 360° clip to show every angle.
Here's the ClozDesign workflow ↓

配套链接（可以附在原帖末尾）：
https://www.cloz-design.com/tools/3d-clothing-mockup-generator?utm_source=x&utm_medium=organic-social&utm_campaign=export-workflow&utm_content=01-image-video

30 秒教程分镜：
- 0–3 秒：先展示同一款设计的正反面图片和旋转视频。字幕：One design. Images + video.
- 3–6 秒：实际设计页打开 Export。字幕：Open Export.
- 6–13 秒：Images → Front + back → White → PNG → 2048 px → Export image。字幕：1. Export your front + back image.
- 13–23 秒：Video → 360° rotation → 10s → 1080p → White → 1:1 → Export video。字幕：2. Export a 360° clip.
- 23–27 秒：展示真实下载结果。字幕：Use both to present your design.
- 27–30 秒：网址与行动提示。字幕：Explore the workflow at cloz-design.com.

制作要求：真实屏幕录制；加速等待过程时注明；不声称全过程只需 30 秒。使用同一设计、同一背景，让图片与视频关联清晰。最终成片先检查服装是否被裁切、字幕是否遮挡关键按钮、下载文件是否能播放。

## 内容 2：让背面设计被看见

英文帖文：
The back of your design deserves a closer look.
In ClozDesign, use Export → Images → Front + back to show both sides together.
Then try Video → Front + back for a moving preview.
Which view helps you judge a design best?

配套链接：
https://www.cloz-design.com/tools/front-and-back-t-shirt-mockup?utm_source=x&utm_medium=organic-social&utm_campaign=export-workflow&utm_content=02-front-back

20–25 秒教程分镜：
- 0–3 秒：展示实际有背部图案的服装，先背面再正面。字幕：Don't hide the back design.
- 3–10 秒：Export → Images → Front + back → PNG，展示导出图片。
- 10–18 秒：Video → Front + back → 5s → 1080p，展示实际导出流程。
- 18–25 秒：并排展示图片与视频结果，结尾放网址。

素材要求：先确认案例确实带背部设计，不用没有背部图案的示例支持这一主题。

A. 对方询问如何清晰展示衣服前后设计：
A front + back image could help people compare the placement on both sides. I'd keep the scale and background consistent so the artwork is easier to judge.

B. 对方展示背部图案但只有单张局部图片：
Would you show a full back view too? I'd like to see how the graphic sits relative to the shoulders and hem.
仅在确实想看完整背面、原帖未已提供该视角时使用。

C. 对方明确询问 3D mockup 工具：
We build ClozDesign. It has front + back image exports and rotating video previews for apparel designs. Happy to share a short workflow if that fits what you need.
明确品牌关联；先确认实际需求匹配。不冒充独立用户推荐。

D. 对方讨论设计展示视频的取景：
I'd start with a full front view, pause on the artwork, then show the back. That makes it easier to judge both placement and the overall silhouette.
